🎯 What This Does

Tracks NASDAQ's biggest components (QQQ, AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, etc.) and generates:

  • Momentum Scores (0-100) - Real-time strength indicator
  • Entry Zones - Optimal buy levels with support/resistance
  • Volume Surge Detection - Unusual activity alerts
  • Opportunity Ratings - EXCEPTIONAL / HIGH / MODERATE / LOW
  • Auto Stop Loss & Targets - Complete risk management

🚀 How to Use

  1. Clone the repo: git clone https://github.com/leoburton8-cmd/nasdaq-opportunity-indicator.git
  2. Get API key: https://www.perplexity.ai/settings/api
  3. Add your key to indicator.py (line ~190)
  4. Run: python indicator.py
  5. Get live signals with entry/exit points!

💡 Pro Tips

Best times to scan: 9:30-10:30 AM ET (market open) or 3:00-4:00 PM ET (close)
Look for: Scores 75+ with ELEVATED or SURGE volume
Avoid: HOLD signals (40-59 score) - wait for better setups
Always use: The suggested stop loss levels for risk management
